Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit the CogniTracer website or contact us.

1. Data Controller

This is a personal, non-commercial website for a proof of concept
Email: hello@cognitracer.com

2. No Cookies Used

We operate this website with a privacy-first approach. We do not set cookies, local storage, or session cookies on your device. Therefore, no cookie consent banner is required.

3. Web Hosting & Server Logs (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA).

When you visit this website, GitHub's infrastructure automatically collects and processes technical log data necessary to serve web pages and maintain system security. This includes:

  • Your IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Referrer URL

Legal Basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest) to deliver the site safely and protect infrastructure against malicious activity.

Data Transfers: Data processed by GitHub may be stored on servers in the United States under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) and GitHub's Privacy Policy.

4. Privacy-Focused Web Analytics (Cloudflare)

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics (provided by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) to understand aggregated page views and site performance.

Cloudflare Web Analytics operates completely without cookies, local storage, or cross-site tracking. When your browser loads the analytics script (static.cloudflareinsights.com), Cloudflare temporarily processes technical request data (such as your IP address and user-agent string) to generate non-identifiable, aggregated usage reports.

Legal Basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest) to analyze overall website traffic and maintain system performance.

Data Transfers: Cloudflare processes network metadata globally and transfers data to the US under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Cloudflare’s Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

5. Email Communication

If you email us directly (e.g., requesting early access via hello@cognitracer.com), we collect your email address and message contents solely to respond to your request. We do not sell or share this information.

Legal Basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (Pre-contractual steps) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest).

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under European data protection law, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you voluntarily send us via email. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@cognitracer.com.
