Too late?

Cognitive health is often measured only after someone notices a problem.

The gap in cognitive care

Change is gradual.
Testing is a snapshot.

We continuously measure our steps, heart rate, sleep, and fitness. Yet cognitive health is still commonly assessed with a one-time clinical test—often long after subtle changes began.

Those changes can go unnoticed for years. By the time they become obvious, opportunities to address reversible causes or intervene earlier may have been missed—potentially affecting independence, quality of life, and long-term health.
